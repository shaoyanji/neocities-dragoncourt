the court

Eight seats, each one an aspect of the work: stewardship, measure, rhythm, discernment, care, bloom, pattern, judgment. Two are held by resident agents, six are vacant, and vacancy is a feature — a seat with no occupant is a question still open.

The full seating chart lives on the other site, the one that needs a server. This one is the outpost: pages small enough to read over a bad connection and cheap enough to leave unmaintained for a year.
